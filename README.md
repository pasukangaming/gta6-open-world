Solara Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game: explore a coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and finish five story missions.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move, W/S to drive, E to steal a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, P to pause. Your cash and best score are saved in the browser.
